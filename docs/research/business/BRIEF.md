# Business research round (10 Oct 2026) – shared brief for all five agents

## What SpiroBuddy is (do not re-research this)
A low-cost base unit that an ordinary incentive spirometer (the plastic breathing exerciser given after surgery) stands on. A laser distance sensor reads how high the disc rises. Software judges each breath, a voice coach speaks in Indian languages, a nurse dashboard shows who is falling behind, and the family gets a WhatsApp update. It coaches and summarises; it never diagnoses.

**Stage, honestly:** working software prototype, live at https://spirobuddy.onrender.com. No hardware built. No real users. No company registered. A student team in India, in the Social Impact Hackathon 2026 (Health & Well-being track), pitching on 11 Oct 2026. The organisers ask whether the team would turn it into a startup.
