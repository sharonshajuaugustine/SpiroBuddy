# Pitch facts (slide-ready)

Rewritten at the end of each round. Only A/B-graded facts unless labelled. Finding IDs (F-…) and ledger numbers (#) point to KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md and SOURCES_LEDGER.md. **Round 2, 9 Oct 2026.**

## The problem
1. **About 1 in 10 adults having surgery gets a lung complication** (10.4% of 9,864 patients, 146 hospitals, 29 countries). LAS VEGAS, *Eur J Anaesthesiol* 2017. **A**. F-CLIN-1
2. **When a lung complication happens, about 1 in 5 patients dies within 30 days: 19.5% vs 0.5% without one** (2,464 patients, Spain). Canet et al., *Anesthesiology* 2010. **A**. An association, not proof of cause. F-CLIN-2
3. **In India too: in a Udaipur study, patients with lung complications stayed 21.7 days in hospital instead of 12.5, spent 8.5 days in ICU instead of 1.4, and 28% of them died.** *Int Surg J* 2018. **A** (small, single centre). F-CLIN-8
4. **86% of US nurses and respiratory therapists say patients' spirometer adherence is poor. The top reason is that patients forget (83.5%); 74% say patients don't use it properly.** Eltorai et al., *Respir Care* 2018, 1,681 respondents. **A**. F-ADH-2
5. **In India: 1 in 4 cancer-surgery patients in Hyderabad couldn't do the exercise correctly, mostly because they didn't understand the instructions or lacked family support.** *Anesth Essays Res* 2018, 100 patients. **A** (small study). F-ADH-4

## Why India needs a different design
6. **Only 41% of older Indians own a smartphone, and only 13% use the internet.** So SpiroBuddy works on the device with a voice coach, and sends WhatsApp updates to the family. HelpAge India report 2025 (via Deccan Herald). **B**, single source. F-INDIA-5
7. **Hindi is the mother tongue of only 43.6% of Indians** (Census 2011), **and 57% of Indian internet users prefer content in their own language** (IAMAI–Kantar 2024). **B**. F-INDIA-8, F-INDIA-6
8. **India has 2.6 registered nursing staff per 1,000 people, below the WHO benchmark of 3, with far fewer in the north and east.** Rajya Sabha reply, July 2026. **B** (news report of a government reply). F-INDIA-7
9. **Heart bypass (CABG) is one of the top 5 procedures under Ayushman Bharat**: the same patients as in the reminder trial below. Lok Sabha reply 2019. **B**. F-INDIA-9

## Proof that tracking and reminders help
10. **An hourly reminder doubled the share of waking hours in which patients did their breathing exercise: 58% vs 28%** (randomised trial, 145 heart-bypass patients, USA). Eltorai et al., *JAMA Surgery* 2019. **A**. F-ADH-1. *Say "hours", not "patients".*
11. **The same trial: breaths per day doubled (35 vs 17), with signs of less lung collapse on X-ray.** **A**. F-ADH-1
12. **Penn's digital spirometer with reminders: about 15 sessions a day, and more sessions were linked to shorter hospital stays** (30 lung-surgery patients, no comparison group). NCT06629454 + AATS 2025. **A/B**. F-ADH-3
13. **A 2024 review of 9 trials (1,209 lung-cancer patients): spirometry as part of a breathing-exercise bundle cut lung complications and shortened hospital stays.** *BMC Pulm Med* 2024. **A**. F-CLIN-6

## The idea is proven, but not for India
14. **The FDA cleared InSee, a clip-on spirometer tracker, on 21 August 2025 (K241152). It reads the piston with an infrared sensor (like ours), fits only one US spirometer and is cleared for hospital use only.** FDA database; IEEE Spectrum 2020. **A/B**. F-COMP-1, F-COMP-5
15. **Positioning line:** "InSee proved laser-tracking of a spirometer works in US hospitals. SpiroBuddy adds what India needs: AI that checks *how* you breathe, a voice coach in Indian languages, home use, and WhatsApp updates for the family."

## How the exercise should be done (justifies our scoring)
16. **Standard instructions: breathe in slowly, hold 3–5 seconds, about 10 breaths every waking hour.** MedlinePlus 2025 and Cleveland Clinic 2026. **B**, two sources agree. F-CLIN-5
17. **The US guideline suggests a volume-type device (what SpiroBuddy reads), used together with deep breathing, coughing, walking and pain control.** An Indian trial (Mangalore 2016) found the volume type gave better walking-test gains. AARC 2011; *J Clin Diagn Res* 2016. **A**. F-CLIN-4, F-CLIN-9

## Cost and feasibility in India

19. **A WhatsApp update to a family in India costs about 12 paise** (utility message ₹0.115, from 1 Oct 2026). Meta rate card. **B**. F-COMMS-2
20. **Voice in 11 Indian languages: Sarvam AI at ₹3 per 1,000 characters (about 60 paise per coaching message), with ₹100 free credits. A free offline backup (AI4Bharat Indic-TTS, 13 languages, MIT licence) also exists.** **B**. F-VOICE-1–3, F-VOICE-10

## Honest limits (say them before judges ask)
21. **The evidence that incentive spirometry alone prevents complications is weak** (Cochrane 2014; a 2024 cardiac-surgery review found it no better than standard breathing care). **But those trials rarely recorded whether patients actually did the exercise. SpiroBuddy makes that visible.** **A**. F-CLIN-3, F-CLIN-7
22. **It's an exercise coach and tracker, not a diagnostic.** The AI voice says it is an AI, and a nurse reviews flags (this also follows Sarvam's terms). Never say "prevents pneumonia" or "first in the world". F-VOICE-8
23. **Automated phone reminders in India need consent records and an Indian-registered caller under TRAI's 2026 rules**, so phone calls are on our roadmap with an Indian telephony partner. WhatsApp and the device beep work today. **B**. F-COMMS-3
