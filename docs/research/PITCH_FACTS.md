# Pitch facts (slide-ready)

Rewritten at the end of each round. Only A/B-graded facts unless labelled. Finding IDs (F-…) and ledger numbers (#) point to KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md and SOURCES_LEDGER.md. **Round 6, 10 Oct 2026.** Judge Q&A: see the "JUDGE" tables in KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md (J1–J35).

## The problem
1. **About 1 in 10 adults having surgery gets a lung complication** (10.4% of 9,864 patients, 146 hospitals, 29 countries). LAS VEGAS, *Eur J Anaesthesiol* 2017. **A**. F-CLIN-1
2. **When a lung complication happens, about 1 in 5 patients dies within 30 days: 19.5% vs 0.5% without one** (2,464 patients, Spain). Canet et al., *Anesthesiology* 2010. **A**. This shows a link, not proof of cause. F-CLIN-2
3. **In India too: in a Udaipur study, patients with lung complications stayed 21.7 days in hospital instead of 12.5 and 8.5 days in ICU instead of 1.4, and 28% of them died.** *Int Surg J* 2018. **A** (small, single centre). F-CLIN-8
4. **About 1 in 8 heart-bypass patients is back in hospital within 30 days (12.9%, 53 studies, 8.9 million patients), and lung problems are among the main reasons.** *J Cardiothorac Surg* 2021. **A** (mostly Western data; no Indian CABG figure found). **In India, after major abdominal cancer surgery, about 1 in 20 (4.7%) is back within a month** (30 centres, 810 patients, *Indian J Anaesth* 2026). **A**. F-BIZ-8, F-BIZ-10
5. **86% of US nurses and respiratory therapists say patients don't use their spirometer as they should. The top reason: patients forget (83.5%).** Eltorai et al., *Respir Care* 2018, 1,681 respondents. **A**. F-ADH-2
6. **In a Chennai hospital, only 6% of 60 patients did the exercise properly on the first day after surgery; 54% did it poorly. With teaching, 72% got it right by day 3.** *J Pharm Bioallied Sci* 2024. **A** (small). F-ADH-6
7. **In Hyderabad, 1 in 4 cancer-surgery patients couldn't do the exercise correctly, mostly because they didn't understand the instructions or lacked family support.** *Anesth Essays Res* 2018, 100 patients. **A** (small). F-ADH-4

## Why India needs a different design
8. **Three in four older Indian women can't read (literacy 24%), and over a third of older Indians have low vision (37%).** So SpiroBuddy speaks. LASI Wave 1 (government survey). **A**. F-INDLOC-3
9. **Two in three older Indians live with their children; only about 6% live alone.** So the WhatsApp update goes to the family. LASI Wave 1. **A**. F-INDLOC-3
10. **The phone is in the house (87% of households have a mobile), but 29% of over-60s have difficulty making a phone call, rising to 35% of older women.** So the patient never has to dial or type. LASI Wave 1 India Report. **A**. F-INDLOC-7
11. **India's own voice-call service (Kilkari, 10 million+ users) reaches half of people on the first call and three in four by the third.** So we retry up to 3 times and back up with WhatsApp. *BMJ Glob Health* 2021. **A**. F-INDLOC-6
12. **Bringing the family in works: in an Indian randomised pilot (Tata Memorial unit, Bihar), supporting caregivers through a Hindi website and a WhatsApp group halved unplanned follow-up visits (1.2 vs 2.6) and lowered family stress.** *Indian J Cancer* 2026, 75 patients. **A** (pilot; complications unchanged). F-ADH-7
13. **Only 41% of older Indians own a smartphone.** HelpAge India 2025 (via Deccan Herald). **B**, single source. F-INDIA-5
14. **Hindi is the mother tongue of only 43.6% of Indians**, and 57% of Indian internet users prefer their own language. Census 2011; IAMAI–Kantar 2024. **B**. F-INDIA-8, F-INDIA-6
15. **India has 2.6 registered nursing staff per 1,000 people, below the WHO benchmark of 3.** Rajya Sabha reply, July 2026. **B**. F-INDIA-7

## Proof that tracking and reminders help
16. **An hourly reminder doubled the share of waking hours in which patients did their breathing exercise: 58% vs 28%** (randomised trial, 145 heart-bypass patients, USA). Eltorai et al., *JAMA Surgery* 2019. **A**. F-ADH-1. *Say "hours", not "patients".*
17. **The same trial: breaths per day doubled (35 vs 17), with slightly less lung collapse on X-ray.** **A**. F-ADH-1
18. **A 2024 review of 9 trials (1,209 lung-cancer patients): spirometry used as part of a breathing-exercise bundle cut lung complications and shortened hospital stays.** *BMC Pulm Med* 2024. **A**. F-CLIN-6
19. **Home lung rehab with phone follow-up works in India: 246 of 260 TB survivors at 5 centres completed 12 weeks, with better walking distance and quality of life.** *Indian J Med Res* 2025. **A** (no comparison group). F-VIS-1
20. **Older rural Indians keep it up: in the Thar Desert, 76% of COPD patients (mean age 64) were still exercising after 8 weeks of phone-guided Hindi rehab, and 58% after a year.** *Lung India* 2025, 45 patients. **A** (feasibility). F-ADH-9

## The idea is proven, but not for India
21. **The FDA cleared InSee, a clip-on spirometer tracker, on 21 August 2025 (K241152). It reads the piston with an infrared sensor (like ours), fits only one US spirometer and is cleared for hospital use only.** FDA database; IEEE Spectrum 2020. **A/B**. F-COMP-1, F-COMP-5
22. **Indian hospitals want AI-assisted follow-up: Amrita (Kochi) is running a trial of a nurse-led AI chatbot with an ECG patch after bypass surgery. It needs a smartphone and doesn't track breathing exercise.** *JMIR Res Protoc* 2026. **A** (protocol). F-COMP-12
23. **Positioning line:** "InSee proved laser tracking of a spirometer works in US hospitals. SpiroBuddy adds what India needs: AI that checks *how* you breathe, a voice coach in Indian languages, home use, and WhatsApp updates for the family."

## How the exercise should be done (justifies our scoring)
24. **Standard instructions: breathe in slowly, hold 3–5 seconds, about 10 breaths every waking hour.** MedlinePlus 2025 and Cleveland Clinic 2026. **B**, two sources agree. F-CLIN-5
25. **The US guideline suggests a volume-type device (what SpiroBuddy reads), used together with deep breathing, coughing, walking and pain control.** An Indian trial (Mangalore 2016) found the volume type gave better walking-test gains. AARC 2011; *J Clin Diagn Res* 2016. **A**. F-CLIN-4, F-CLIN-9

## Money, who pays, and how big
26. **Ayushman Bharat pays hospitals a flat ₹1,62,400 for a bypass (CABG), with a 10-day stay built in. Any extra days from a complication come out of the hospital's pocket.** HBP 2022 rate list (state copy). **A**. F-BIZ-1
27. **What a hospital day costs in India: about ₹2,307 per ward bed-day on average (57 hospitals, 11 states) and ₹25,927 per ICU bed-day at PGIMER.** CHSI, *BMJ Open* 2023; *Lung India* 2024. **A**. F-BIZ-4, F-BIZ-5. *Back-of-envelope only:* a week of extra ICU ≈ up to ₹2 lakh. Label it as our estimate.
28. **Indian chains already pay for home monitoring after surgery** (Manipal + ConnectedLife, 2022, for bypass and knee replacement patients). **B**. F-BIZ-3
29. **A WhatsApp update costs about 12 paise (₹0.115, Meta's India utility rate). An AI phone call costs about ₹8 for 2 minutes, roughly 70 times more.** So: WhatsApp first, calls only to escalate. Meta rate card; Plivo + Sarvam prices (our maths). **B**. F-COMMS-2, F-COMMS-11
30. **Voice in 11 Indian languages: Sarvam at ₹3 per 1,000 characters (about 60 paise per coaching message), with ₹100 free credits. A free offline backup (AI4Bharat Indic-TTS, MIT licence) also exists.** **B**. F-VOICE-1–3, F-VOICE-10
31. **Unit economics (team estimate): about ₹1,000–1,700 of parts per reusable clip-on (≈ ₹150 a patient over 10 uses) and about ₹5 of WhatsApp per 14-day recovery. Price idea: ₹500–1,000 per patient, less than half a ward day and roughly one home physiotherapy visit (from about ₹500, Grade C).** F-BIZ-7 (not a source), F-BIZ-9
32. **The next market: India notifies about 26 lakh TB patients a year and 90% finish treatment successfully (PIB, March 2026). In Madurai, 59% of patients finishing treatment still had impaired lungs. Our rough estimate: lakhs of TB survivors a year could use home breathing rehab.** NTEP asks for every one of them to be checked, and **India's 2024 RBIPMT pulmonary-rehab guidance (draft, on ntep.in) lists the incentive spirometer among its breathing exercises.** Over 20% of India's TB is outside the lungs (WHO 2025), so say "lakhs", not an exact number. **A** (estimate labelled). F-VIS-4, F-VIS-5, F-VIS-6, F-VIS-2, F-VIS-3

## Honest limits (say them before judges ask)
33. **The evidence that incentive spirometry alone prevents complications is weak** (Cochrane 2014; a 2024 cardiac-surgery review found it no better than standard breathing care). **But those trials rarely recorded whether patients actually did the exercise. SpiroBuddy makes that visible.** **A**. F-CLIN-3, F-CLIN-7
34. **Digital reminders alone help only modestly (TB review of 76 studies: OR 1.14, low certainty), but digital follow-up does bring problems to the care team sooner (side-effect reporting OR 1.57).** That's why we pair sensor data with nurse alerts. *BMC Infect Dis* 2025. **A**. F-ADH-8
35. **Regulation: CDSCO lists the plain incentive spirometer as Class A (lowest risk, non-measuring, "not a diagnostic device"), needing only registration. Our clip-on *measures* and *alerts*, so it will need its own registration and probably a licence (Class A measuring or Class B).** CDSCO Class A list (2025) and software guidance (2026). **A**. F-REG-5, F-REG-4
36. **Calls: TRAI's 2026 rules treat AI voice calls as "A2P calls". They need advance declaration, an Indian registered caller ID and, for some reminder calls, consent the patient renews weekly.** So calls are an escalation step: the nurse clicks "call" and a human talks to the patient. **A**. F-COMMS-8, F-COMMS-12, F-COMMS-13
37. **Accuracy: the sensor chip is ±2 cm out of the box but very steady, so we calibrate it to each spirometer's printed marks and narrow its view (27° → about 15°) so tube walls don't confuse it.** ST datasheet; ST forum; Thai study 2026. **A/B/C**. F-TECH-1, F-TECH-2, F-TECH-3

Never say: "prevents pneumonia", "first in the world", "first AI follow-up in India" (Amrita), "laser sensing is our invention", "diagnoses", "reduces readmissions" (not measured; knee readmissions are ~1%), "₹X saved per patient", "CDSCO-approved", "no licence needed", "no regulation applies", "elderly Indians prefer voice" (no trial; use the literacy data instead), "no Indian readmission data" (there is: 4.7% after abdominal cancer surgery), "no TB guideline uses incentive spirometry" (RBIPMT 2024 does), "the TB programme recommends home spirometry" (the guidance is for supervised centre sessions), "AI calls change behaviour" (Kilkari RCT: no effect on its main outcome).
