# Pitch facts (slide-ready)

Rewritten at the end of each round. Only A/B-graded facts unless labelled. Finding IDs (F-…) and ledger numbers (#) point to KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md and SOURCES_LEDGER.md. Round 1, 9 Oct 2026.

## The problem
1. **About 1 in 10 adults having surgery gets a lung complication** (10.4% of 9,864 patients, 146 hospitals, 29 countries). LAS VEGAS, *Eur J Anaesthesiol* 2017. **A**. F-CLIN-1
2. **When a lung complication happens, about 1 in 5 patients dies within 30 days: 19.5% vs 0.5% without one** (2,464 patients, Spain). Canet et al., *Anesthesiology* 2010. **A**. Single source; an association, not proof of cause. F-CLIN-2
3. **86% of US nurses and respiratory therapists say patients' spirometer adherence is poor. The top reason is that patients forget (83.5%); 74% say patients don't use it properly.** Eltorai et al., *Respir Care* 2018, 1,681 respondents. **A**. F-ADH-2
4. **In India: 1 in 4 cancer-surgery patients in Hyderabad couldn't do the exercise correctly, mostly because they didn't understand the instructions or lacked family support.** *Anesth Essays Res* 2018, 100 patients. **A** (small study). F-ADH-4

## Proof that tracking and reminders help
5. **An hourly reminder doubled the share of waking hours in which patients did their breathing exercise: 58% vs 28%** (randomised trial, 145 heart-bypass patients, USA). Eltorai et al., *JAMA Surgery* 2019. **A**. F-ADH-1. *Say "hours", not "patients".*
6. **The same trial: breaths per day doubled (35 vs 17), with signs of less lung collapse on X-ray.** **A**. F-ADH-1 (small effect: 1.5 vs 1.8 on a 10-point score)
7. **Penn's digital spirometer with reminders: patients did about 15 sessions a day (14.7), and more sessions were linked to shorter hospital stays** (30 lung-surgery patients, no comparison group). ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06629454 + AATS abstract 2025. **A/B**. F-ADH-3
8. **An optical clip-on for ordinary spirometers can measure volume to within about 7.5% and classify breaths with 99–100% accuracy** (University of Michigan, lab study). *JMIR Biomed Eng* 2023. **A**. F-COMP-4

## The idea is proven, but not for India
9. **The FDA cleared a clip-on spirometer tracker, InSee, on 21 August 2025 (K241152, Class II). It fits only one US spirometer (Vyaire AirLife) and is cleared for hospital use only.** FDA 510(k) database. **A**. F-COMP-1
10. **Positioning line:** "InSee proved the clip-on idea in US hospitals. SpiroBuddy brings it home, for Indian spirometers, in Indian languages, with WhatsApp updates for families."

## How the exercise should be done (justifies our scoring)
11. **Standard instructions: breathe in slowly, hold 3–5 seconds, about 10 breaths every waking hour.** MedlinePlus (US NLM, 2025) and Cleveland Clinic (2026). **B**, two sources agree. F-CLIN-5
12. **The US respiratory-care guideline suggests a volume-type device** (which is what SpiroBuddy reads) **and says to use it together with deep breathing, coughing, walking and pain control.** AARC guideline, *Respir Care* 2011. **A**. F-CLIN-4

## Cost and feasibility in India
13. **A volumetric spirometer (Romsons Breazer 2500) sells online for about ₹200–₹1,000 (printed MRP about ₹1,067).** Retailer pages. **C**, label it. F-INDIA-3
14. **A WhatsApp update to a family in India costs about 12 paise (utility message, ₹0.115; from 1 Oct 2026).** Meta rate card. **B**. F-COMMS-2
15. **Indian-language voice: Sarvam AI speaks 10 Indian languages plus English, at ₹3 per 1,000 characters, with ₹100 of free credits.** A 3-sentence coaching message costs about 60 paise (our maths). Sarvam docs/pricing 2026. **B**. F-VOICE-1–3

## Honest limits (say them before judges ask)
16. **The evidence that incentive spirometry itself prevents complications is weak** (Cochrane 2014: 12 trials, low-quality evidence, no proven benefit). **But those trials never recorded whether patients actually did the exercise. SpiroBuddy produces exactly that missing data.** **A**. F-CLIN-3
17. **It's an exercise coach and tracker, not a diagnostic.** We say "helps patients do the exercise doctors prescribe", never "prevents pneumonia".
