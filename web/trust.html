    4. What we do not claim

    Designed to follow these guidelines. Not reviewed or approved by ICMR, an ethics committee or CDSCO.

    • SpiroBuddy has not been tested on real patients, and no hospital has used it.
    • It is a working preview with sample patients. It is not a medical device and it is not for clinical use.
    • We make no claim that it prevents complications or changes how many patients return to hospital. A proper study would have to show that.
